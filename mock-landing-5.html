Resonant features

Pop-up shelter map

Instant tiles show spots offering overnight respite, plus the volunteers meeting people there.

Sound bites from shelter volunteers, musicians, and people waiting for apartments share what housing feels like.

Storehouse ledger

Donors broadcast what they bring—coffee, blankets, sleeping bags—so needs and drops match in real time.

Moodboard idea: a rotating collage of flyers and patchwork fabric backgrounds that show the tactile reality of each shelter.
